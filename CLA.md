# OMEM Individual Contributor License Agreement

> **Before you rely on this document:** it is adapted from the Apache Software
> Foundation's Individual Contributor License Agreement v2.0, which is the form
> most open-source CLAs derive from. It has **not** been reviewed by counsel for
> this project, and the entity named below does not exist yet. Have a lawyer
> review it, and fill in the entity, before enabling CLA enforcement on the
> repository. Until then this file documents the intent.

## Why this exists

OMEM is MIT licensed and **the core will stay MIT**. This agreement does not
change that and does not take your rights away. You keep full ownership of
everything you write and can use it anywhere else you like.

What it does is let the project accept your contribution with clear provenance,
and keep open the option of building a commercially licensed product alongside
the MIT core. Without a CLA, that option would later require tracking down every
contributor for permission, which in practice means it never happens.

If you would rather not sign, that is a legitimate position and we would still
like your help. Open an issue describing the change and it will be implemented
with credit to you. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## How to sign

Comment on your pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

The bot records your signature against your GitHub username. You sign once, not
per pull request.

**Contributing on behalf of an employer?** You likely need a Corporate CLA
instead. Open an issue and we will sort it out before you spend time on a patch.
